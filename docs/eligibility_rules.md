Eligibility Rules – Vaccination Scheduling and Cold-Chain Engine

1. Introduction

Eligibility rules determine whether a beneficiary can proceed with vaccination scheduling based on the configured conditions of the system.

2. General Eligibility Rules

1. Beneficiary details must be registered before checking eligibility.
2. Required beneficiary information must be available.
3. The beneficiary must satisfy the configured eligibility conditions.
4. Previous dose information must be checked before scheduling another dose.
5. The required dose sequence must be followed.
6. Invalid or incomplete beneficiary information must be rejected.
7. A beneficiary who does not satisfy the conditions must not proceed to scheduling.

3. Dose Eligibility Rules

- Verify the beneficiary's previous vaccination records.
- Check the required dose number.
- Check the configured interval between doses.
- Prevent duplicate dose records.
- Update the vaccination status after a successful dose record.

4. Scheduling Eligibility

- Only eligible beneficiaries can proceed to appointment scheduling.
- Appointment details must be valid.
- Scheduling conflicts must be checked.
- Vaccine availability must be considered.

5. Validation

The system should validate beneficiary details, dose information, and scheduling inputs before processing.

6. Conclusion

Eligibility rules help maintain accurate beneficiary records and support a consistent vaccination scheduling process. All vaccine-specific conditions must be configurable according to the applicable project requirements.
